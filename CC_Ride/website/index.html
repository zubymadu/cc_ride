Corporate-assured security

Built to survive a security audit, not just a demo

The two things typical ride-sharing treats as an afterthought — driver identity and trip traceability — are the two things CC Ride is built around.

Verified driver identity

Every driver's identity is confirmed against real government ID (NIN or passport) before they can carry a passenger — no exceptions.

Every trip, traceable

OTP-confirmed pickup and drop-off, plus a full trip record — exactly who drove, who rode, when, and where.

Access scoped to who shares it

Shared pool vehicles are only visible to employees of the owning company — or a partner organisation it's explicitly agreed to share with.

Transparent, upfront pricing

Every fare is shown before booking — no surge surprises, no hidden fees, a clean audit trail for every naira spent.
